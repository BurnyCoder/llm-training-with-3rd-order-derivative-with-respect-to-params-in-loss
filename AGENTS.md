# Research conventions

Follow the published preregistration, preserve exact split and checkpoint provenance, and qualify claims by their direct evidence. Make data or analysis changes explicit in the experiment history. Hold the test sets until the registered comparisons are frozen. Pin reproducible dependencies with uv and keep credentials, raw corpora and optimizer checkpoints out of Git. The paper alone carries the AI statement.

Publish the source and tests before the first run. Each completed training or evaluation updates raw records, study reports, checkpoint cards and the public explorer. Use one review pass for scientific, correctness and maintenance risks and retain negative results. [Protocol](reports/preregistration.md) · [Planned runs](reports/planned-runs.md)
