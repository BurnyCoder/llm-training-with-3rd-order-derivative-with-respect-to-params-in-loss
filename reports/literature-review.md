# Literature review

**Question.** Does explicitly discouraging variation of the parameter Hessian improve out-of-sample next-token predictions? The search covered parameter-Hessian penalties, high-order derivative regularization, tensor norm estimation, automatic differentiation, compact LM benchmarks and public implementations. Input derivatives, optimizer approximations, and parameter-loss derivatives answer different questions.

| Source | What it establishes | Decision for this study |
|---|---|---|
| [Duchon, 1977, pp. 85–87](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0086566) | Integrating sums of squared derivatives gives rotation-invariant roughness functionals. | Apply the local order-three density at training weights. Spline interpolation theorems do not carry over to neural training. |
| [Lellmann, Morel and Schönlieb](https://www.lellmann.net/work/_media/lellmann_ssvm13.pdf) | Third-order regularizers for reconstructing surfaces use mixed partials. Isotropic smoothing can erase features on their elevation data. | Compare predictive loss independently of any reduction in curvature variation. Their total-variation penalty has a different power and integration domain. |
| [Liu, Yu and Lin, equations 9–12 and §5.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.05924) | The paper defines cross-entropy plus a signed Hessian trace and reports WikiText-2 LSTM and GRU experiments. Algorithm 1 uses dense Rademacher probes. The language experiments use the sparsified variant. | Adapt the dense equation to causal Transformers. Published recurrent-model scores are external results. |
| [Authors’ trace implementation](https://github.com/Dean-lyc/Hessian-Regularization/blob/7d18787b06ba3e7912bf841ea9ebe4505a6191b3/hess.py#L87-L98) | In this public code path the trace is converted to a Python number. Adding it to cross-entropy cannot transmit its parameter gradient. | Test optimizer motion and the penalty gradient. The observation concerns this released implementation path. |
| [Bujanović and Kressner, especially §3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.06433) | Products of independent sign vectors support unbiased trace estimation with structured probes. The randomness has different concentration properties than a full independent probe. | Unfold the order-three tensor along one index, apply a rank-one probe and square the resulting vector norm. Preserve independent probes and log stochastic variability. |
| [Wolinski, ECML PKDD](https://ecmlpkdd-storage.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/preprints/2025/research/preprint_ecml_pkdd_2025_research_332.pdf) | Grouped directional derivatives make higher-order parameter information accessible for optimization. | Compute directional contractions but use their magnitude as a term in the training objective. |
| [Liu et al., ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/liu23ao.html) | Language-model runs can have similar pretraining losses and different downstream behavior. | Limit the main claim to within-domain next-token prediction. |
| [Dinh et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/dinh17b.html) | Reparameterization can alter local geometry while preserving the predictor. | Interpret geometry in the fixed training coordinates. Compare methods within each model stage. |
| [Eldan and Li](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.07759) | Synthetic English stories allow small causal models to exhibit meaningful language behavior. | Use full-weight pretraining with fixed-prompt, uncurated continuations. |
| [Pythia](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.01373), [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.09685), [Common Pile](https://huggingface.co/datasets/common-pile/arxiv_abstracts_filtered) | An open pretrained decoder, low-rank adaptation and a dated abstracts collection support a second-domain experiment. | Restrict derivatives to the trained QKV adapters. Use first-submission months after the Pythia release. |
| [Bouthillier et al.](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/0184b0cd3cfb185989f858a1d9f5c1eb-Abstract.html), [Lones](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.02497), [NeurIPS checklist](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist) | Evaluation needs explicit variability, data isolation, reporting and reproducibility. | Pair seeds, fix the estimand and tuning budget, and report individual runs and both comparisons. |
