# Research plan

The accepted design is preserved in the [preregistration](preregistration.md) and [run matrix](planned-runs.md). Work proceeds from public registration to source and test publication, analytical verification, development pilots, secondary controls, paired full-budget confirmations, sealed evaluation and reporting. Publish code, raw summaries, model cards and figures throughout. The live dashboard tracks development metrics and exposes checkpoint progress. The paper and interactive explorer will report positive, negative and inconclusive outcomes from the same raw results.
