# Adapter-coordinate numerical verification

Before interpreting adapter training, we checked the same GPT-NeoX, LayerNorm and PEFT modules used by the experiment on a small offline backbone. The check differentiates the squared sampled third derivative along a fixed joint displacement of the two trainable QKV factors. It also recovers the perturbed logits from an exported adapter loaded into a fresh copy of the backbone. [Executable check](../../tests/test_derivatives.py) · [PEFT checkpoint format](https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/developer_guides/checkpoint)

The first version used a single close pair of parameter displacements. The finite difference disagreed with autograd at the test tolerance. Inspection of GPT-NeoX showed that eager attention explicitly evaluates softmax in single precision even with double-precision parameters. A sweep of displacement sizes showed loss of accuracy at the smallest displacements and truncation error at the largest. The revised oracle cancels the leading central-difference error using two larger displacements. Its tolerance and the model code stayed the same. The verification record archives the autograd and finite-difference values.
