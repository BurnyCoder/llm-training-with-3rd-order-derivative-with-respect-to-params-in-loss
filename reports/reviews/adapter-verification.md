# Adapter-coordinate numerical verification

Before interpreting adapter training, we checked the same GPT-NeoX, LayerNorm and PEFT modules used by the experiment on a small offline backbone. The check differentiates the squared sampled third derivative along a fixed joint displacement of the two trainable QKV factors. It also recovers the perturbed logits from an exported adapter loaded into a fresh copy of the backbone. [Executable check](../../tests/test_derivatives.py) · [PEFT checkpoint format](https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/developer_guides/checkpoint)

The first version used a single close pair of parameter displacements. The finite difference disagreed with autograd at the test tolerance. Inspection of GPT-NeoX showed that eager attention explicitly evaluates softmax in single precision even with double-precision parameters. A sweep of displacement sizes showed loss of accuracy at the smallest displacements and truncation error at the largest. The revised oracle cancels the leading central-difference error using two larger displacements. Its tolerance and the model code stayed the same. The verification record archives the autograd and finite-difference values. [Upstream eager attention](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/main/src/transformers/models/gpt_neox/modeling_gpt_neox.py) · [Numerical differentiation and step size](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.differentiate.derivative.html) · [Recorded checks](../../results/verification/tests.json)

## First pretrained calibration interruption

The first probe of the pretrained model produced finite adapter-gradient coordinates but an infinite single-precision norm. A replay of the same training windows and random signs found that the sum of squares overflowed. The implementation widens the norm reduction to double precision when the ordinary result is infinite or zero. Scores, calibration draws, gates and tuning ratios keep their registered definitions. The original measurements include the number of nonfinite coordinates, both norms, elapsed time, and peak device allocations. [Replay record](../../results/verification/pythia-calibration-replay.json) · [PyTorch norm accumulation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.linalg.vector_norm.html)

The joint computation also allocated more memory than the device’s dedicated VRAM. The adapter path now differentiates the two windows sequentially. For fixed probes, write their contractions as r₀ and r₁ and their mean as R. The derivative of the squared norm of R with respect to each rᵢ equals R. Propagating this cotangent through each window’s third-derivative graph and summing the resulting fourth-order contributions includes the cross-window terms. The two windows use identical probes, frozen weights during the calculation, and deterministic forwards. The verification suite compares the scalar and every gradient coordinate against a joint calculation in double precision.
