# Pre-execution scientific review

The preregistration tests a claim about held-out next-token prediction within two fixed stage-specific protocols. This review records failure modes and diagnostic checks before the first model run. Empirical conclusions require the recorded final evaluations. [Registered estimand and comparisons](../preregistration.md)

## Mathematical target

The third tensor is the derivative of **unregularized mean token cross-entropy** in the trainable coordinate system. Keeping two independent sign probes fixed through all differentiations yields a vector contraction. Its squared length is unbiased for the squared Frobenius norm conditional on that particular minibatch. A shared probe leaves cross terms. Checking the mixed cubic by enumerating the two sign vectors exposes this error, whereas a single-coordinate quartic catches a detached fourth derivative. The dense reference uses three nested forward Jacobians and central differences of the dense contraction. [Rank-one norm estimation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.06433) · [PyTorch gradient checks](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/gradcheck.html)

Squaring after averaging across documents makes the objective depend on the probe-window batch size. Ordinary gradient accumulation can preserve token cross-entropy while changing this penalty. The two probe windows must share one graph and keep the same target count. The physical CE microbatch can vary independently. [Registered batch definition](../preregistration.md)

## Interpretation of the intervention

Signed Hessian trace and squared tensor norm differ in both derivative order and scalar aggregation. The squared-Hessian control helps distinguish these changes. Coordinatewise randomization of the penalty-gradient signs preserves the absolute values of its entries before addition, clipping and AdamW. It will generally change their relationship to the ordinary gradient and the optimizer's moments. A comparison supports a contribution from directional information under this optimizer. An independent conclusion about the origin of generalization would require further interventions. [Hessian comparator](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.05924) · [Reparameterization example](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/dinh17b.html)

In the adapter stage, the derivatives are in the two low-rank factors. Factor rescaling can leave their product unchanged while changing derivatives in the factor coordinates. Treat an adapter result as a replication of the training recipe in that parameterization. The change of corpus and checkpoint precludes isolating model scale as the sole cause of a stage difference. [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.09685)

The inverse gate-probability multiplier restores the expected sampled penalty gradient before clipping. Clipping and adaptive moment normalization are nonlinear, so the resulting parameter update need not be an unbiased estimate of an update under dense regularization. Report instability and the relationship between the penalty gradient and the ordinary gradient where recorded. [Derivation and gating schedule](../preregistration.md)

## Inference and leakage

Use the paired seed as the independent unit. Report individual NLL differences alongside the sign count. The one-sided sign test conditional on non-ties assesses win probability. A few large adverse effects can therefore coexist with many small wins. Show median effects and exact binomial order-statistic intervals, with the achieved coverage exposed because discreteness can make them conservative. Holm adjustment covers the two primary hypotheses within a stage. The elapsed-training-time comparison uses the last recorded evaluation on the same fixed development windows at or before each seed's common cutoff. It reports the time gap for each arm. The full development split measures final validation performance. [Sign test](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.binomtest.html) · [Distribution-free quantile intervals](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/TechnicalNotes/NIST.TN.2119.pdf) · [Holm procedure](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/stats/html/p.adjust.html)

The report withholds primary deltas and p-values until it has all final equal-token records. The evaluator must also freeze the entire checkpoint set before scoring. Hash the evaluation split and frozen set, and retain every dev candidate and each through-training stopping decision. Corpus cleaning can miss paraphrases or boilerplate even after exact and shingle matching. A later source timestamp reduces one route of pretraining overlap while leaving coincidental or previously circulated text possible. Report corpus provenance and review all split audits.
